Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-11-05

This Privacy Policy explains how TripleMonkey Studio ("we") collect, use, and share your information when you use our iOS application, Memory Pirates ("the App").

What information do we collect?

We do not collect any personally identifiable information ("PII") from you when you use the App. However, we may collect certain non-PII information, such as your device identifier and device model. We also use Game Center and iCloud to persist data, which may include your Game Center username.

How do we use your information?

We use the non-PII information we collect to improve the App and to provide you with a better user experience. We also use Game Center and iCloud to persist data so that you can continue playing the game where you left off on any of your devices.

How do we share your information?

We do not share your information with any third parties, except as required by law or to provide you with the services you have requested. For example, we may share your Game Center username with other players on the Game Center Leaderboards.

How do we protect your information?

We take all reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. We use a variety of security measures, including encryption and firewalls, to protect your data.

Your choices

You can choose to not use the App, or you can choose to not use Game Center or iCloud to persist data. You can also delete your Game Center account or disable iCloud synchronization for the App.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make any material changes, we will notify you by posting the updated Privacy Policy on the App's website.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@triplemonkey.studio.

Still Need Help?

If you run into any issues, have feature requests, or want to share feedback, I'd love to hear from you! Complete the form below and include your email, and I will return your message as soon as possible. You can also write to support@TripleMonkey.studio.
