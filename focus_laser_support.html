Getting Started

What Is Focus Laser?

Focus Laser is designed to help you strip away digital distractions, track deep work sessions, and maintain total clarity throughout your day.

Quick Setup Guide

Block distracting apps:

  1. Go to the Settings tab (bottom right) and tap Grant Access under Improve Your Focus.
  2. Tap Continue in the popup window and follow the prompts to grant Screen Time access permissions.
  3. Tap the toggle switch next to Block Apps During Focus to enable app blocking.
  4. Tap Select Apps to Block to open a list of app and categories to blcok.
  5. Tap on the circle to the left of the cagories or app names to blcok their access during focus sessions.

Start a focus session:

  1. Tap the desired preset focus length or the custom timer above the 'Start Focus' button.
  2. Tap the Start Focus button to begin a focus session.

Widgets & Live Activities

Focus Laser includes Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets to help you keep track of your active focus session at a glance.

Adding a Widget to Your Home Screen

  1. Touch and hold an empty area on your iPhone Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap the + button in the top corner.
  3. Search for Focus Laser and tap on it.
  4. Select your preferred widget size and tap 'Add Widget'.

Widget Troubleshooting

If your widget is not updating or displays blank:

  • Restart the App: Open Focus Laser, force close it (swipe up from the App Switcher), and reopen it.
  • Re-add the Widget: Remove the widget from your Home Screen, restart your device, and add the widget back.
  • Check Background App Refresh: Go to iOS Settings > General > Background App Refresh and ensure it is turned ON for Focus Laser.

In-App Purchase of Pro Membership

Focus Laser offers Pro membership for a one-time purchase granting access to all pro featueres for the life of the application.

Purchasing Pro Membership

  1. Open the Settings tab in the Focus Laser App.
  2. Tap the UPGRADE TO PRO button near the top and follow the prompts to complete the purchase.

Restoring Previous Pro Purchase

If you reinstalled the app or upgraded to a new iPhone and your Pro features aren't showing up:

  1. Open Focus Laser.
  2. Go to the Settings menu inside the app.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.
  4. Sign in with the Apple ID used for the original purchase if prompted.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Focus Laser does not collect any personal information. Using the app does not give TripleMonkey Studio access to any information about you or your device.
